Today I have released the first version of [Delt06/unity-pix](https://github.com/Delt06/unity-pix) - a convenient PIX integration into the Unity Editor. UX-wise, it is similar to the built-in RenderDoc integration, which has been available in Unity for quite some time already.

At work or when tinkering with pet projects, I tend to use multiple GPU debugger/profilers apart from RenderDoc: mostly PIX and Nsight Graphics. Even since [Delt06/aaaa-rp](https://github.com/Delt06/aaaa-rp) I have felt a need to integrate these tools right into the Unity Editor since RenderDoc was not always enough. That's precisely why I have decided to a develop a plugin for PIX. Below is a brief description of use cases when one might favor it over RenderDoc.

- The biggest reason for having PIX is an ability to quickly iterate during optimization sessions. PIX reports both timings and hardware-specific metrics, making it a pleasure to test optimization hypotheses.
  - ![Timings](/assets/blog/2025-06-28-unity-pix-timings.jpg)
  - ![Unit Throughputs](/assets/blog/2025-06-28-unity-pix-unit-throughputs.jpg)
  - ![SM Instruction Execution Throughputs](/assets/blog/2025-06-28-unity-pix-sm-instruction-execution-throughputs.jpg)
- Another one is DXIL shader debugging. As of summer 2025, RenderDoc has only partial support for it (see [changelog for RenderDoc v1.36](https://github.com/baldurk/renderdoc/releases/tag/v1.36)). Whether you actually use ShaderModel 6.0+ or simply compile with DXC for compilation speed and stability, PIX has been my to-go tool for shader debugging.
  - ![PIX Shader Debugging](/assets/blog/2025-06-28-unity-pix-shader-debugging.jpg)

Even though the use cases above are quite important, PIX might fall short in other debugging scenarios because, unfortunately, it is slow, clumsy, and may be somewhat unstable on bigger projects. Therefore, it is better to keep all the tools in mind.

If you want to give PIX in Unity a shot, I suggest trying out [Delt06/unity-pix](https://github.com/Delt06/unity-pix), it might improve your debugging and profiling workflows.
